About CivicPulse

Bridging the gap between citizens and government through transparency and accountability.

Our Mission

To empower every Nigerian citizen to report civic issues and hold government accountable, while providing government with the tools to respond effectively and transparently.

📢

Report

Easily report bad roads, broken lights, flooding, waste, corruption, and water issues with photo and location.

👀

Track

Follow the status of your reports – Reported, Under Review, In Progress, Resolved – and see government responses.

🏆

Transform

Drive change through transparency scores, community upvotes, and documented government action.

Our Story

CivicPulse was founded in 2025 by a team of civic technologists who saw the need for a simple, transparent way for Nigerians to report everyday problems. Instead of waiting for government to notice, citizens can now document issues and start a public conversation.

We believe that accountability begins with data. Every report, every response, every resolution is tracked and visible. This creates a virtuous cycle – government sees what matters, citizens see action.

Our Vision

To become the default civic engagement platform for Nigerian cities – a digital common where every citizen's voice is heard and every government action is transparent.

We aim to expand to all 36 states, integrate with local government systems, and provide real‑time dashboards for officials.
